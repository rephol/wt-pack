# Settled decisions

A plan entry labelled `settled:` records a decision the user already made, usually against a named
alternative. It is not yours to improve.

## What the label does

- **Scopes to the labelled entry only.** Details the plan leaves open remain your judgement.
- **Does not suppress defect evidence.** A real defect discovered inside a settled approach is reported at
  full strength. The label governs preference, never facts.
- **Turns disagreement into a blocker, not a workaround.** If implementation proves the decision unworkable —
  infeasible, the wrong thing, destructive — that is a genuine blocker. Report it. Do not silently work
  around it, and do not "fix" the decision.

## What it is not

It is not a claim that the decision is correct. It is a claim that it was **decided**, with its alternative
seen and rejected. Re-litigating it costs the user the conversation they already had.

## The test

Before deviating from a settled decision, ask: *am I saying this cannot work, or that I would have chosen
differently?* The first is a blocker worth raising. The second is not a finding.
